    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough, and there are no online trophies. Roughly 200 hours at PowerPyx's 4/10 difficulty. Work the stages in order — the story first, then side content, then cleanup — and keep a separate save at the start and end of every chapter, because there is no mission replay. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off your platinum save.
